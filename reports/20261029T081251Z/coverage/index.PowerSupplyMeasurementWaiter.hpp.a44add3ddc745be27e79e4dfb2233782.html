14 /// \brief Waits for the power supply measurements to satisfy a condition during the DC cable check.
15 ///
16 /// During the cable check EvseManager has to wait for the output voltage to reach the isolation test
17 /// voltage, and to drop below the safe voltage again. Both are decided from the measurement stream
18 /// published by the power supply.
19 ///
20 /// That stream is not necessarily fast or regular: depending on the hardware it may originate from a
21 /// power meter that is polled over the network. A gap in it therefore says nothing about the voltage,
22 /// and must not be confused with the voltage failing to move. This class keeps the two apart, and
23 /// keeps waiting for the whole time budget instead of giving up on the first gap.
24 ///
25 /// The class performs no I/O and has no side effects: reading measurements, cancellation and logging
26 /// are all injected by the caller.

61 /// \param total_timeout overall budget for the condition to be satisfied. Note that for the cable
62 /// check this budget sits inside the CableCheck timeout of the EV, so it must not be raised
63 /// carelessly.

65 PowerSupplyMeasurementWaiter(std::chrono::milliseconds total_timeout,
66 std::chrono::milliseconds measurement_timeout);

68 /// \brief Waits until \p condition is satisfied, the budget expires, or waiting is cancelled.
69 ///
70 /// A gap in the measurement stream is not a failure by itself: waiting continues for the
71 /// remaining budget, so a measurement source that goes briefly silent does not abort an otherwise
72 /// healthy cable check. Every gap is reported to \p on_gap and counted in the result, so that the
73 /// caller can report a stalled measurement stream as something other than a voltage that did not
74 /// move.
75 Result wait(const Condition& condition, const MeasurementSource& next_measurement, const CancelledCheck& cancelled,
76 const GapObserver& on_gap) const;
