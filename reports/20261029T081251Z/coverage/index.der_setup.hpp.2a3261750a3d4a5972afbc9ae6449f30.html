28 /// \brief Build the SAE DER transfer limits from the module's AC transfer limits and grid parameters.
29 ///
30 /// Nominal charge comes from the ac_limits charge maximum, and both nominal and max discharge from the
31 /// ac_limits discharge maximum, so the discharge pair satisfies [V2G20-3229] (magnitude compare) and the
32 /// charge pair [V2G20-3228] (plain <=). An absent discharge limit yields zeros. The discharge sign follows
33 /// ac_limits, so it is negative only under the module's negative_bidirectional_limits option, while the AMD1
34 /// replacement of Table 94 (binding via [V2G20-1204]) and [V2G20-1035] require discharge powers <= 0.
35 ///
36 /// Reactive-power limits come from the EVSE's reported capability, as non-negative absorption and
37 /// non-positive injection; the grid limits from its nominal voltage and frequency. Pure and session-free.

39 std::optional<float> evse_max_reactive_power,
40 std::uint32_t nominal_voltage);

66 /// \brief Derive the DER transfer limits for the currently advertised energy services.
67 ///
68 /// Limits are only built for the DER services present in \p services. AC_DER_SAE additionally needs a
69 /// positive nominal frequency and voltage: the voltage is the base for the PercentageV grid code curves, and
70 /// the frequency is the mandatory GridNominalFrequency ([V2G20-3269], Table M.41) the EV adopts in place of
71 /// EVSENominalFrequency ([V2G20-3233]). Either one missing yields GridParametersMissing and no SAE limits,
72 /// never a placeholder grid. The frequency-trip curves are absolute Hz and are not scaled by the nominal
73 /// frequency, so a 60 Hz site needs 60 Hz curve values supplied with the setup config. Pure and
74 /// session-free; the caller decides what to log and what to assign.
75 DerLimitsDerivation derive_der_limits(const std::vector<iso15118::message_20::datatypes::ServiceCategory>& services,
76 const iso15118::d20::AcTransferLimits& ac_limits,
77 std::optional<float> evse_max_reactive_power,
78 std::optional<std::uint32_t> nominal_voltage);
