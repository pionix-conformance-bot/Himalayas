25 // first, then close our side. ISO 15118-20 [V2G20-1776] wants D-LINK_TERMINATE 2 s after the

27 // 5 s of [V2G20-1633]; DIN [V2G-DC-937/938] and -2 share the value. Must stay below the -4 ATS
28 // par_CMN_TCP_Connection_Termination_Timeout of 5 s. Poll-driven: blocking here would stall the
29 // shared SDP server. Negotiation failures and plug-out bypass it entirely ([V2G-DC-940]).
