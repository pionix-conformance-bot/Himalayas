31 /// \brief Whether an ISO 15118 session can ever occur on this EVSE.

37 /// \brief Decides an EVSE's DER controller component from static facts.
38 ///
39 /// \param der_wired Whether a grid_support provider is wired to this EVSE.
40 /// \param connectors The EVSE's connectors as reported by evse_manager, each carrying its own charge mode.
41 ///
42 /// An EVSE gets at most one DER controller, so the connectors' charge modes are collapsed to one answer:
43 /// every connector AC yields Ac, every connector DC yields Dc. Fails closed everywhere else, which is an
44 /// unwired EVSE and an EVSE whose connectors disagree - OCPP cannot express two controllers, and
45 /// provisioning the wrong one is worse than provisioning none.
46 ///
47 /// Ac additionally requires that some connector is hlc_capable, because AC DER exists only as an
48 /// ISO 15118-20 relay. Dc does not check it: EvseManager refuses to start a DC EVSE without HLC, so a DC
49 /// connector reporting no HLC could not have booted.
50 ///
51 /// Deliberately not derived from an EVSE's supported energy transfer modes, which are published
52 /// asynchronously and are not waited on before the device model is built.
53 DerCtrlrComponent der_ctrlr_component(bool der_wired, const std::vector<types::evse_manager::Connector>& connectors);
