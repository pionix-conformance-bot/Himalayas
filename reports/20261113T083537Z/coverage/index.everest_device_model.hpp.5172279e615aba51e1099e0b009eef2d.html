40 /// \brief Whether an ISO 15118 session can ever occur on this EVSE.

46 /// \brief Decides an EVSE's DER controller component from static facts.
47 ///
48 /// \param der_wired Whether a grid_support provider is wired to this EVSE.
49 /// \param connectors The EVSE's connectors as reported by evse_manager, each carrying its own charge mode.
50 ///
51 /// An EVSE gets at most one DER controller, so the connectors' charge modes are collapsed to one answer:
52 /// every connector AC yields Ac, every connector DC yields Dc. Fails closed everywhere else, which is an
53 /// unwired EVSE and an EVSE whose connectors disagree - OCPP cannot express two controllers, and
54 /// provisioning the wrong one is worse than provisioning none.
55 ///
56 /// Ac additionally requires that some connector is hlc_capable, because AC DER exists only as an
57 /// ISO 15118-20 relay. Dc does not check it: EvseManager refuses to start a DC EVSE without HLC, so a DC
58 /// connector reporting no HLC could not have booted.
59 ///
60 /// Deliberately not derived from an EVSE's supported energy transfer modes, which are published
61 /// asynchronously and are not waited on before the device model is built.
62 DerCtrlrComponent der_ctrlr_component(bool der_wired, const std::vector<types::evse_manager::Connector>& connectors);
